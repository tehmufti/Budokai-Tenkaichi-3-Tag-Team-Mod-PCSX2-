Natural and Fighter walking/running animation data
==================================================

The motion samples in tools/ground_motion_cmu.py are adapted from Carnegie
Mellon University Graphics Lab motion capture, using Bruce Hahne's 2010
MotionBuilder-friendly BVH conversion. These animation-data assets have
their own terms below. They are not relicensed under the mod code's GPL-3.0
license and are not CC0/public-domain assets.

The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu.
The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.

Original database and current reuse terms:
https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/

CMU permits using the data in projects and including it in commercial
products, but prohibits selling the motion data itself, including in a
converted form. Please consult the original database for the complete
terms. Bruce Hahne imposes no additional restrictions on his conversion;
the accompanying CMU-READMEFIRST.txt preserves his original explanation
and attribution. That historical file describes the terms as they were
published in 2010; the database's current restriction on selling the motion
data itself also applies to the adapted data supplied with this mod.

BVH conversion:
https://sites.google.com/a/cgspeed.com/cgspeed/motion-capture/cmu-bvh-conversion
Distribution mirror:
https://github.com/una-dinosauria/cmu-mocap

Modifications: retargeted to the game's biped hierarchy, looped and filtered,
repaired knee/elbow bend planes, authored foot contact, adjusted pelvis
height and weight transfer, restrained head movement, and adjusted torso
and arm poses. Fighter adds a stronger lean and arm drive. Motion samples
are normalized to leg length and encoded for the native animation player.
No game mesh, texture, original full capture, or animation from another
commercial game is distributed in these files.
