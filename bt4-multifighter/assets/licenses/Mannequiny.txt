Mannequiny walking and running animation attribution
===================================================

The motion samples in tools/ground_motion.py are adapted from the walk and
run animations in Mannequiny v0.4.0, by GDQuest, Luciano Munoz and contributors.

The source animation was read from the GLB conversion published by
hardboileduser (Blender 4.2 LTS) at:
https://opengameart.org/content/mannequiny-the-open-3d-mannequin
https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/glb-mannequiny-0.4.0.zip
The exporter reports material alpha adjustments, with no motion changes.
Source GLB SHA-256:
84caf77b520980448ca4fb3267b7272ba76fde20c0ce925f7a546c83fa5c01f8

Changes made for this mod: retargeted skeletal rotations and pelvis movement
to the game's skeleton, scaled movement to character proportions, adjusted
foot contact, head posture, walking stance and arm motion, and running torso
lean. The motion was resampled and encoded for the game's animation format.
Only walking and running motion is adapted. The source mannequin model,
textures, preview meshes, and other source animations are not distributed.
